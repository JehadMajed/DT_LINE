NB2 RS485 PORT LOCATION:
=========================
The NB2 smart breaker has an RS485 communication port on the bottom or side.
Look for pins labeled:
  - Pin 3: RS485 A+ (Data+)
  - Pin 4: RS485 B- (Data-)
  - Pin 1/2: May be power or other functions (check your model)


COMMUNICATION SETTINGS:
========================
  Protocol:     Modbus-RTU
  Baud Rate:    19200 (default, configurable at register 0x0101)
  Data Bits:    8
  Parity:       Even (default, configurable at register 0x0102)
  Stop Bits:    1
  Slave Addr:   3 (default, configurable at register 0x0100)


IMPORTANT NOTES:
================
⚠ The NB2 breaker handles AC MAINS VOLTAGE (220V/380V).
  - NEVER touch breaker terminals while energized
  - RS485 A/B wires are low-voltage and isolated from mains
  - Use shielded twisted-pair cable for RS485 (recommended max: 1200m)
  - Add 120Ω termination resistor between A and B at end of long cable runs (>10m)
  - Keep RS485 cable away from power cables to avoid interference

⚡ MAX485 Module Notes:
  - DE (Driver Enable) and RE (Receiver Enable) are tied together
  - When EN=HIGH → Transmit mode (ESP sends data to NB2)
  - When EN=LOW  → Receive mode (ESP receives data from NB2)
  - The firmware handles this automatically via rs485PreTransmission/rs485PostTransmission

🔌 Power:
  - MAX485 module runs on 3.3V from ESP32
  - Some MAX485 modules have 5V logic — use 3.3V compatible version!
  - The NB2 breaker powers its RS485 interface internally from the mains
